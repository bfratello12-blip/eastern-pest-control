Residential pest control

Your house is the biggest thing you'll ever buy. Protecting it shouldn't mean a stranger spraying a baseboard and leaving a door hanger.

Year-round protection

Pests don't take the winter off

In the Northeast, the problem just moves indoors. Mice come in as soon as nights drop below 50°. Overwintering stink bugs settle into attic voids in October. Carpenter ants swarm in the wall cavity in April.

A once-a-year treatment can't keep up with that. Our residential program is built around the region's actual pest calendar — the barrier gets renewed before each pressure window, not after you've already found something in the kitchen.

The Northeast pest calendar

What we're watching for,
season by season

Your plan shifts with the year. Here's roughly what a technician is targeting on each visit.

SPR

March – May

Termite and carpenter ant swarms, overwintering stink bugs waking up, ant colonies foraging indoors, first wasp queens starting nests under eaves.

SUM

June – August

Mosquito and tick pressure peaks, yellow jacket colonies mature, pavement ants and spiders move to shaded exterior harborage.

FAL

September – November

Rodents seeking warmth, stink bugs and boxelder bugs congregating on sunny walls, hornet nests at maximum size, spiders indoors.

WIN

December – February

Mice already inside walls and attics, cluster flies in upper floors, stored-product pests in pantries, ongoing exclusion work.

Safety first

Safe for the people and pets living there

“Is this safe for my kids?” is the first question we get on almost every job, and it deserves a real answer instead of a marketing line.

We use the lowest-risk material that will actually solve the problem — usually a bait or a gel placed in a void your family never touches. When a liquid is necessary, it's applied to cracks, crevices and the exterior perimeter, and it's inert once dry, typically within 30 minutes.

Your technician will tell you exactly what was applied, where, and how long to keep pets off it. Product labels and safety data sheets are available on request for anything we put on your property.
