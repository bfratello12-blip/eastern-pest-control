How it works

Four steps. No surprises.

The same process on every job, whether it's a studio apartment in Hoboken or a 200,000 sq ft distribution center.

01

Inspect & identify

A licensed technician walks the property, confirms the species, finds the harborage and maps every entry point. Inspection fee is waived when you book service.

02

Build the plan

You get a written plan with the materials we'll use, where they go, what it costs and what you should expect week by week. No mystery line items.

03

Treat & exclude

We treat the active problem, then close the door behind it — sealing gaps, correcting conducive conditions and setting monitors where they matter.

04

Monitor & guarantee

Follow-up visits confirm the population is gone. If anything comes back between services, so do we — at no charge.
