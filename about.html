Our story

Built on route work,
not marketing

Ray Castellano started Eastern out of a garage in Mineola, New York, servicing 40 accounts by himself. He'd spent eight years as a technician for a national company and had gotten tired of being told how many stops he had to hit before lunch.

The idea was simple: if you give a technician enough time to actually inspect a property, you solve problems instead of managing them. Fewer callbacks, longer customer relationships, better margins. It took a while for the industry to agree.

More than a decade later we run 14 licensed technicians out of three branches, and Ray's daughter Nina runs operations. We still measure success the same way — by how few second visits we have to make.

What we hold to

Four things we won't trade

Diagnose before you treat

No technician of ours applies a material before they can name the species and point at the source. Guessing is how you get callbacks.

Least material that works

Baits and exclusion before liquids, targeted placement before broadcast. Better for your family, and honestly more effective.

Same face every visit

Routes are built so you keep your technician. They learn your building, and you stop repeating yourself.

Say what you found

Including when it's bad news, and including when the honest answer is that you don't need us.
