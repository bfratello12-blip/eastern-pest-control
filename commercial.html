Why facilities switch to us

A pest problem is a business problem

A failed health inspection closes a dining room. A rodent sighting kills a five-star review. A single stored-product pest in a shipment triggers a supplier audit. The cost of the pest is never the cost of the pest.

Eastern runs commercial accounts on a documented Integrated Pest Management program: sanitation and structural corrections first, monitoring devices mapped and trended, and materials used only where the data says they're needed. Your binder — digital or physical — is always current and always ready to hand to an auditor.

What's in the program

Standard on every commercial account

Digital logbook

Every service, finding and corrective action recorded and timestamped. Auditors get a login; you get the whole history in one place.

Device mapping & trending

Numbered stations on a floor plan, with catch counts trended month over month so you can see a problem forming before it's a problem.

After-hours service

Overnight and pre-open windows so treatment never happens with customers, students or patients in the room.

Dedicated account manager

One named contact who knows your building, your schedule and your auditor. Not a rotating queue.

Staff awareness training

Short on-site sessions for your team on receiving inspection, sanitation standards and what to report immediately.

Emergency response

A 24/7 line that reaches a technician, not a voicemail. Two-hour on-site commitment for critical accounts.

Onboarding

From survey to steady state

Most accounts are fully onboarded within ten business days of the initial survey.

01

Site survey

A full facility walk with your operations lead. We photograph conducive conditions, review any inspection history and identify critical control points.

02

Program design

A written scope: device count and placement, service frequency, materials, escalation thresholds and reporting cadence. Priced per site, not per head office.

03

Installation

Devices placed, numbered and mapped. Logbook created. Staff briefed. Baseline readings taken so future trends mean something.

04

Service & review

Scheduled service on your windows, with quarterly program reviews and an annual scope reassessment as your operation changes.
