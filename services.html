What we treat

Every service, in detail

Most requested

General Pest Control

The backbone of everything we do. A treated barrier at the foundation, de-webbing and nest knockdown at the eaves, and targeted interior work where activity shows up.

  • Ants, spiders, roaches, silverfish, earwigs, crickets and more
  • Crack-and-crevice and void applications — not broadcast spraying
  • Conducive-condition report after every visit
Get a quote

Termite Control & Inspections

Eastern subterranean termites cause more structural damage in this region than fire and storms combined. We inspect, treat and monitor — then put the warranty in writing.

  • Liquid soil barrier or in-ground baiting systems
  • WDI reports for real estate closings, usually within 48 hours
  • Renewable damage warranty on qualifying structures
Get a quote

Rodent Control & Exclusion

Traps alone never solve a mouse problem. We find the quarter-inch gaps they're using, seal them with rodent-proof materials and document every repair with photos.

  • Interior trapping and removal — no poison inside living space
  • Full exterior exclusion with copper mesh, mortar and sheet metal
  • Droppings clean-up and sanitizing of affected areas
Get a quote

Bed Bug Treatment

Fast, discreet and thorough. Canine-assisted inspection confirms where they actually are, then we choose whole-room heat, conventional treatment or a combination.

  • Whole-room thermal remediation to 135°F
  • Two verification visits included in every treatment
  • Unmarked vehicles available on request
Get a quote

Cockroach Extermination

German roaches move through walls, so treating one unit rarely works. We treat the whole harborage — voids, motor housings, hinges — with gel bait rotations that beat resistance.

  • Gel bait and growth regulator rotation
  • Multi-unit coordination for apartments and co-ops
  • Follow-up at 14 and 30 days, included
Get a quote

Mosquito & Tick Control

A seasonal program that treats where they rest, not where you sit. Monthly applications April through October, with larvicide in any standing water we can't drain.

  • Barrier treatment of shrubs, tree lines and shaded harborage
  • Tick tubes and perimeter granular for deer tick pressure
  • One-time event treatments for weddings and parties
Get a quote

Wasps, Hornets & Bees

Same-day nest removal for yellow jackets, bald-faced hornets and paper wasps. Honey bee colonies are referred to a local keeper for live relocation whenever it's possible.

  • Soffit, wall void and in-ground nest treatment
  • Physical nest removal and entry sealing
  • Live honey bee relocation referrals
Get a quote

Wildlife & Attic Exclusion

Squirrels, raccoons, bats and birds get in through roof lines, ridge vents and chimney chases. We remove them humanely, close the openings and restore what they ruined.

  • One-way doors and humane live removal
  • Roof line, vent and chimney exclusion with steel mesh
  • Attic sanitizing and insulation replacement
Get a quote
